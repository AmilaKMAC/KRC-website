About Us

History

Kurunduwatte Royal College (KRC) in Gampola was founded on March 26, 2009, with just seven teachers and 160 students. Since its establishment, the school has grown substantially to accommodate over 1,000 students with approximately 70 teachers, offering education from grades 6 through 13 in Sinhala, Tamil, and English mediums. Despite being a relatively young institution, KRC has quickly become one of the leading schools in the Central Province, establishing a strong presence in the region.

Achievement

Academically, KRC has gained recognition for its impressive O-Level examination results and performance in scholarship programs. The school also emphasizes extracurricular activities, hosting the annual “Battle of the Hills” cricket match, maintaining active Naval Cadet and scouting programs, and encouraging students to participate in inter-school competitions. These achievements highlight KRC’s commitment to fostering not only intellectual growth but also physical fitness, teamwork, and leadership skills.

Values

The school’s motto, “Best amongst the best!”, reflects its aspiration for excellence. KRC’s vision is to produce productive, efficient, and disciplined citizens, aligning with its mission to nurture well-rounded individuals who contribute positively to society. With its core values of discipline, leadership, community engagement, and holistic development, the school fosters an environment where students excel academically while growing personally and socially.
