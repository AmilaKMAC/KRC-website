“At Kurunduwatte Royal College, we believe in providing a balanced education that nurtures academic excellence, creativity, and character development. Our academic programs are designed to prepare students for future success in higher education and beyond.”

Kurunduwatte Royal College (KRC), Gampola, follows the national curriculum of Sri Lanka, preparing students for the Ordinary Level (O-Level) and Advanced Level (A-Level) examinations. The school offers a wide range of subjects across science, commerce, and arts streams, including Mathematics, Physics, Chemistry, Biology, Information Technology, English, Sinhala, Tamil, History, Geography, and Economics. In addition to academics, KRC provides special programs such as Naval Cadet training, scouting, sports teams, cultural activities, and leadership workshops, ensuring students receive a well-rounded education.
