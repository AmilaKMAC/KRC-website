Admission to Kurunduwatte Royal College

Join our school to experience quality education from Grade 6 to A-Level (Grade 13) in a supportive and dynamic learning environment.

How to Apply

The admission process is simple and transparent. Follow the steps below to apply for your child’s enrollment:

1
Submit Application

Fill out the online application form or obtain a physical form from the school office.

2
Entrance Test

Applicants will sit for a placement/entrance test according to the grade applied for.

3
Interview

Shortlisted candidates and their parents will be invited for an interview with the school panel.

4
Admission Confirmation

Successful applicants will receive the admission confirmation along with fee details and next steps.

Required Documents

  • Birth Certificate of the student
  • Previous School Report / School Leaving Certificate
  • Parent/Guardian NIC
  • Passport-size photos (2 copies)
  • O-Level / A-Level Results (if applicable)

Important Information

  • Applications are accepted during the designated admission period each year.
  • Students from Sinhala, Tamil, and English mediums are welcome.
  • Merit-based and need-based scholarships may be available.
  • Ensure all documents are submitted before the deadline.
